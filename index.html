σ_NMAD (lower = better) is the robust scatter; η is the catastrophic-outlier fraction. Predictions saturate at z≈2 because <3% of DESI DR1 lies above z=2 and the strong redshift-bearing lines leave DESI's 3600–9800 Å window beyond z≈2 — so the high-z bins are undertrained and the expected-value decode caps out there.

Generated from the DR1 val split (965,897 spectra) and an SDSS OOD sample (23,419). Tokenizer v2, encoder–decoder transformer (~100M params).
